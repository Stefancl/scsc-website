What Happens After Your Photoshoot? Editing, Galleries and Delivery Explained

A simple look at what happens between the end of an SCSC photography session and receiving your finished online gallery.

SCSC Journal

Practical photography guidance for families, people and businesses across Somerset West, Stellenbosch and the Cape Winelands.

View session details ↗

The photographs are first selected

A session produces more frames than the final gallery needs. We review the images carefully, removing duplicates, test frames, missed expressions and anything that does not add to the story.

Editing creates consistency

Selected images are adjusted for exposure, colour, contrast and overall tone so that the gallery feels coherent. Editing is about finishing the photograph, not making people look like different versions of themselves.

Why delivery takes a few days

Careful selection and editing take time. Standard SCSC family sessions are delivered within 5–7 working days. Larger events and commercial projects may have different timelines depending on the volume of images and project requirements.

Your private online gallery

Finished photographs are delivered through a private online gallery. This allows clients to view the collection as a complete story and download high-resolution and social-ready files.

Back up the photographs you love

Online galleries are convenient delivery tools, but important family photographs should also be backed up. Keep copies in more than one place, ideally using both local storage and a cloud service.
